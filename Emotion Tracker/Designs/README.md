# Emotion Tracker — UI/UX Design

A mobile UI/UX design concept focused on helping users check in with their emotions, record their moods, and understand their emotional patterns through a simple and approachable interface.

## 🎨 Project Overview

The Emotion Tracker design explores a user-friendly way to make emotional check-ins feel simple and engaging.

The design includes different screens for:

* Emotion selection and mood check-ins
* Daily mood tracking
* Personalized home experience
* Mood-related content and suggestions
* Emotional trends and visualizations
* User profile and personalization

## 📱 Design Screens

### Emotion Check-In

Users can select how they are feeling through a simple emotion-based interface.

### Mood Tracking

The interface allows users to record and view their emotional states over time.

### Home & Personalized Content

A personalized home experience brings together mood-related information and content in one place.

### Mood Insights

Visual elements are used to represent emotional patterns and trends, making the tracked information easier to understand.

## 🎯 Design Goal

The goal was to create an emotion-tracking experience that feels approachable, visually engaging, and easy to navigate while giving users a way to reflect on their emotional patterns.
